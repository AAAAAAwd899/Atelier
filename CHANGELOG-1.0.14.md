# Atelier 1.0.14

All seventeen world tiles now respond with finite scene motion and lighting. Forest-like worlds sway, water worlds drift, night worlds glow and warm worlds gently turn. Hover and keyboard focus share the response; selected choices have a checkmark flourish. Targets, labels and grid positions remain steady. Reduced motion and Atelier motion-off disable decorative animation.

Every existing world, artwork file, saved choice and original add-on function is retained. No new artwork, dependency, engine feature or publication channel.
