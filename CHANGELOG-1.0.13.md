# Atelier 1.0.13

Folder appearance now presents an open collection of authored objects. Repeated fieldset, preview and tile borders are removed. Selected objects retain a soft wash and checkmark.

Artwork sways, peeks or turns through a small orbit on hover and keyboard focus, then settles after two cycles. A saved choice briefly greets in the preview. Labels and hit targets do not move. Reduced-motion and Atelier motion-off remain static.

All existing icon choices, folder colors, artwork, organization, authoring, review, exams, remotes and compatibility paths are retained. No new media or dependency.
