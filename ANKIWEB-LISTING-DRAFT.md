# Atelier — Study, Create & Organize

A calmer, more complete home for Anki. Keep your classes easy to find, build cards from the material you study, prepare for exams, and choose a world that feels like your own space.

## One place for everyday study

Keep Add cards, Browse, and Sync close at hand. Navigate existing decks as folders, preview lectures before opening them, save favorites, and focus on the classes that matter now without deleting or suspending old cards.

## Create with your sources beside you

Card Studio brings documents, figures, drafts, and image masks into one workflow. Review the destination, duplicates, and proposed notes before adding cards to Anki. Source projects and your Anki deck library stay clearly separate. Original source images are retained for masking and publishing; smaller previews make browsing lighter.

## Prepare and practice

Exam Studio helps organize dates, decks, and study routines. Build independent practice sessions or answer-locked mock exams, record confidence and written responses, save an unfinished session, and review results. Pip can rehearse an existing active study group without requiring a duplicate plan.

Independent practice does not change normal Anki scheduling. Pip's optional **Introduce new cards** mode is separate and can submit real ratings for eligible new cards after reveal. A saved timed exam's deadline continues while closed. Self-marks and planning indicators are not predictions of a real exam grade.

## Make it your space

Choose from twelve coordinated worlds. Built-in widgets can show your activity, active groups, upcoming exams, unfinished work, recent material, or a personal note. Put selected widgets on the Study desk too. Onigiri is optional; its own rewards remain available through a separate, clearly labelled route.

## Install and update

Version **1.0.0** targets **Windows desktop Anki 26.08.1 with the standard editor**. Experimental editors, beta Anki releases, other desktop platforms, and arbitrary third-party combinations are not advertised as verified targets. It is not an AnkiMobile, AnkiDroid, or AnkiWeb interface add-on.

The supplied direct-file installer uses Tools → Add-ons → Install from file. Install over earlier Atelier, keep standalone Exam Sprout and Workshop disabled, and fully restart. Do not delete their data. When distributing through an AnkiWeb listing, use that listing's real installation instructions and verify migration from the direct-file identity before directing existing users to it.

## Your data

No telemetry or automatic diagnostic upload. Ordinary creation and study need no AI service. Optional provider assistance requires configuration and approval before outgoing text is sent; suggestions remain drafts. Service policies and charges apply.

Anki Sync does not include all local source projects, exam records, widget notes, or Atelier settings. Back up the collection, Atelier's `user_files`, and the profile's complete `exam_sprout` and `workshop` folders. Local add-on data is not encrypted by Atelier. The included guide explains backup and recovery.
