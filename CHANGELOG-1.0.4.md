# Atelier 1.0.4

- The expanded optional Onigiri rewards grid now releases full-page row positions and heights inside the Study desk. Favorites and activity sit directly after the metric cards instead of below a blank band.
- Saved widget widths are respected responsively, including a wide activity tile beside Favorites. The original Onigiri nodes, controls, and saved layout are restored when leaving the embedded view.
- The browser regression fixture now gives its placeholder tiles readable padding and explicitly labels its missing live restaurant and heatmap. It is test evidence, not a native Onigiri capture.
- The active-study folder picker puts recently observed folders and folders containing newer-created cards first, including parent branches. Search and multi-parent selection still work. Its search focus outline now follows the outer field, and the narrow-window heading and exam-date field align with the list.

All 1.0.3 features and the existing private add-on identity remain in the cumulative installer.
