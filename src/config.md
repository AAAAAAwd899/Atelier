# Atelier 1.0 — Study, create, and organize

For appearance settings, use **Tools → Atelier → Appearance & compatibility** instead of editing this file. Choose a world, adjust spacing and motion, and decide how your reviewer and supported Anki tool windows look.

**Widgets → Arrange widgets** customizes the Widgets page. Select **Show on Study desk** to put a widget on the main home too. Choose **Save layout** to keep changes. Onigiri is not required.

Exam Studio and Card Studio are included. Keep separate Exam Sprout and Workshop installations disabled, without deleting their data. Your existing plans, sources, drafts, and widget settings are kept.

**Sync updates your Anki collection, not all local Atelier data.** Back up Atelier's `user_files` plus your Anki profile's `exam_sprout` and complete `workshop` folders. See **USER-GUIDE.md** for everyday workflows, backups, and recovery.

Normal review uses your Anki schedule. Independent practice does not reschedule cards. Pip's explicitly enabled **Introduce new cards** mode can submit real ratings for eligible new cards after reveal.

Atelier 1.0.1 targets Windows desktop Anki 26.08.1. Specialized templates and other add-ons may need their own settings. Use the original-reviewer recovery action when required; do not remove your data to change appearance.

Remote controls are off by default. Use Tools → Atelier → Remote controls to test and assign buttons; do not hand-edit key codes. Pair in system Bluetooth settings first.
