# Atelier 1.0.8

- Move folders and decks through a searchable destination browser instead of a long dropdown. Browse one level at a time, use the path to go back, or choose All folders to move out of nesting.
- Preview the original and final paths before confirming. A folder and its descendants move together. Self/descendant destinations and filtered decks are excluded; unchanged locations and duplicate names receive an explanation.
- Find Move directly in the folder right-click menu or through the existing three-dot Organize menu. Existing drag-and-drop still opens confirmation.
- After a native move, open the destination reliably through Anki's page redraw. Native Undo remains available in Edit.
- Preserve all existing features, artwork, stores and engine code. No automatic reorganization, merging, bulk move or scheduling engine was added.
