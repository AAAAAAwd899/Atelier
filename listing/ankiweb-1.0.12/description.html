Atelier brings your classes, card creation and study into one calm Anki workspace.

YOUR LIBRARY, OPENED UP
Open a course and all its lectures on the main screen. Keep your own order for courses and for each course's lectures. Choose icons and colors, pin favorites, and set an exam from an existing folder. Combine several parent folders in one exam without moving or merging cards.

MAKE CARDS WITH YOUR MATERIAL BESIDE YOU
Card Studio organizes slides, PDFs, images and notes into source projects. Make questions, clozes, image cards and image-occlusion masks, then review drafts and their destination before adding them to Anki. The normal Anki editor remains available for your other note types and tools.

CHOOSE WHAT TO STUDY
Continue a lecture, review a whole folder, or keep several exams in focus. Native whole-folder shuffle preserves lecture context while Anki manages scheduling and daily limits. Exam Studio offers separate practice and mock sessions, saved unfinished work, self-marking and results. Pip can use your focused folders. Its optional Introduce new cards mode is identified before starting because it can submit real Anki ratings. Study indicators are planning aids, not predictions of an exam grade.

MAKE IT FEEL LIKE YOUR SPACE
Seventeen scenic worlds, twenty-four illustrated folder symbols, six folder colors, built-in widgets, a study clock and a guided tour. Controls give clear selection feedback without moving their targets, and reduced motion is respected. Optional Onigiri rewards can stay on the Study desk while Onigiri retains its own progress and controls. Original-theme companion mode remains available.

INSTALLATION AND COMPATIBILITY
Use Anki’s Add-ons manager to install Atelier, then fully restart. For a manual .ankiaddon file, choose Tools > Add-ons > Install from file. Minimum declared Anki: 25.09.5. Current native functional checks used Anki 26.09.3 on Windows with an offscreen test profile. Physical display/GPU, macOS/Linux, hardware remotes and every other add-on combination are not universally verified. This is a desktop add-on, not an AnkiMobile, AnkiDroid or AnkiWeb interface.

DATA AND BACKUP
Atelier does not automatically upload source documents or diagnostics. Ordinary card creation needs no AI service. Optional provider assistance requires configuration and approval before outgoing text is sent; suggestions remain drafts. Keep separate backups of your collection, Atelier user_files and the complete profile-local exam_sprout and workshop folders. Anki Sync does not include every local source project, exam record or widget setting.
