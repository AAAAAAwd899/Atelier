A calmer workspace for your Anki decks, cards and exams.
Atelier brings your classes, source material, card creation and study choices into one desktop workspace. Keep lectures organized, choose exactly what to study, and prepare for an exam across several folders without merging your decks.

Organize your study
Keep courses and lecture decks in their original folders, with recent and saved shortcuts close by.
Save a study focus containing one lecture, a whole class or several parent folders. Overlapping branches count once.
Study a folder with native ordering options, including mixing eligible cards across lectures. Anki's scheduling limits still apply.
Build an exam plan from multiple folders, then use practice sessions, mocks and saved practice.
Make cards beside your sources
Card Studio organizes imported slides, PDFs, images, documents and text into projects. Work through an ordered page rail, draft focused questions, make cloze or image cards, or draw image-occlusion masks over a figure. Review the drafts and destination before adding cards to Anki.
Add cards and Browse keep Anki's native editor and browser available for your note types and specialized editor tools. Source projects and drafts remain separate from your published cards.

Find a study space that suits you
Choose from 17 scenic worlds, adjust density and motion, arrange home widgets, or use optional Pip Study. A small themed study clock offers a countdown or stopwatch with pause, resume and reset. It stays available while you review or create cards. The clock does not change schedules or Anki's study-time statistics.
If you use the separate Onigiri rewards add-on, its live restaurant and reward controls can appear on the Study desk. Onigiri is optional and is not included.

Compatibility and data
This is an independent add-on for
Anki desktop 25.09.5 or newer
. Current native checks were run on
Windows with Anki 26.09.3
. macOS, Linux, physical remotes, live GPU Pip and every third-party add-on combination have not been verified. It does not run on AnkiWeb, AnkiMobile or AnkiDroid.
Other add-ons keep their own menus and windows; they are not all embedded into Atelier. Original appearance options remain available for compatibility. The assessment and authoring engines are bundled: keep separate Exam Sprout and Workshop installations disabled.
There is no telemetry or automatic upload. Local projects, exam plans, masks, preferences and clock state need separate backups; Anki Sync and deck exports do not include all of them. Optional provider-assisted authoring requires its separate explicit setup and consent. Back up your collection and local work before updating.
If you installed an earlier Atelier file directly, read the update instructions on GitHub before adding an AnkiWeb copy. Avoid running duplicate Atelier installations.
