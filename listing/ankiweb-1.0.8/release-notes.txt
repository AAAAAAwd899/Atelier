Atelier 1.0.8

Folder reorganization now uses a searchable destination browser. Browse one level at a time, use the path to go back, or choose All folders to move out of nesting. Check From and To before confirming. Folder contents move together; cards and their review history stay intact.

Move is available directly in the right-click menu and through the existing Organize menu. Drag-and-drop still asks for confirmation. Duplicate names and unchanged destinations receive a clear explanation. After a move, the destination opens reliably through Anki's redraw. Use Anki's native Edit > Undo to reverse the move.

This cumulative update retains original add-on menu tools, the themed clock and stopwatch, guided tour, task help, all seventeen worlds, image occlusion and specialized native editor access, multi-parent exams, source projects, masks, saved sessions, remotes and optional widgets. No universal third-party compatibility or untested-platform claim is made.
